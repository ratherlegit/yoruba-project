# Songs Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (the author chose native execution). Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** A Songs section: a list of Yoruba songs with a genre filter, and a page per song with an embedded YouTube video, a few lyric lines each with an English meaning, and a link to the full published lyrics.

**Architecture:** Typed content in `src/content/songs.ts` with a validator (same pattern as proverbs and numbers), two pages (`/songs`, `/songs/$songId`), a seventh landing card with a new icon, a nav link. No stats, no audio, no database.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React, TanStack Router, Vitest + Testing Library, Playwright.

**Spec:** none written, by the author's choice. The design was agreed in chat on 2026-10-20 and is recorded in the Global Constraints below.

## Global Constraints

- Copyright: at most 8 lines of lyrics per song, always with a link to the full lyrics on the published page the lines came from. Never show a full lyric sheet.
- The Yoruba lines are copied as published on that page (published pages often omit tone marks and dots; the song page says so). Normalised to NFC. The English meaning under each line is written by the author of this code, not copied from any translation site.
- A song enters the list only when a published lyrics page was found for it.
- All song content is `confidence: "needs-review"` with a `source` and a `note` naming the doubt, listed in `content/REVIEW.md` (`npm run review`).
- No em dashes (`src/no-em-dash.test.ts`), no eyebrow labels, no environmental impact figures. No stray `ɡ` (U+0261).
- Video is embedded with `youtube-nocookie.com`, `referrerPolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"`, with a plain "Watch on YouTube" link beside it as a fallback.
- Sacred subjects are left out (Boju Boju is excluded: it is about the Oro masquerade).
- The icon (a ṣẹ̀kẹ̀rẹ̀, beaded gourd rattle, from the agreed pool) must be shown to the author as a screenshot before merge.
- Do not merge or push without the author's go-ahead.

## Review Focus

- A song whose video is removed or blocks embedding still shows its lyrics and the YouTube link.
- An unknown song id shows a not-found card with a way back, not a blank page.
- A genre filter that matches nothing cannot be reached (only genres in use are offered).
- External links open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
