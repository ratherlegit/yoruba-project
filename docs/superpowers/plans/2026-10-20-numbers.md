# Numbers Section Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add a dedicated Numbers section (`/numbers`, six steps, each with an explanation and typed practice), move the vocab topic "Numbers one to ten" into it, and record results for the stats page and the landing card.

**Architecture:** A sourced content table (`src/content/numbers.ts`) with an arithmetic-checking validator, a pure practice builder and grader on top of `gradeTransform`, an extension of the `yp:stats:v1` store, two pages and a practice component reusing `YorubaKeys` and `insertChar`, and a sixth landing card with a new icon.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, React, TanStack Router, Vitest + Testing Library, Playwright, `tsx` scripts.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-numbers-design.md`. This branch (`numbers`) starts from `landing-stats`.

## Global Constraints

- No em dashes (U+2014), no eyebrow labels, no environmental impact figures. All Yoruba strings NFC with tone marks, and no `ɡ` (U+0261): use the normal `g`.
- Every number entry and phrase has `confidence: "needs-review"`, a `source` naming at least two sources, and a `note` naming the doubt. Nothing is marked verified.
- Number words are copied from sources, never computed. The `build` expression is only checked for arithmetic.
- Typed answers are graded with `gradeTransform` (easy ignores dots and tones, advanced needs them exact); digit answers compare as plain digits after removing spaces and commas.
- No new audio is recorded. A "hear it" question exists only for a number whose Yoruba text has a clip (`audioUrlFor` returns a url).
- Stats rules from the landing-stats work still hold: only counts, ids and dates are stored; recording happens only in event handlers; everything works when storage is blocked; no zeros, NaN or percentages on an empty state.
- Vocab: the topic `gs-numbers` is removed from `src/content/vocab/getting-started.ts`. A saved `gs-numbers` entry in `yp:vocab-progress:v1` is ignored.

## Review Focus

- A valid variant typed by the learner (for example oókànlá for 11, or ení for 1) must be accepted (Tasks 2, 3).
- No accepted answer may equal an accepted answer of a different number (Task 1 validator, Task 2 data).
- `build` arithmetic equals `value` for every entry (Task 1 validator, Task 2 data).
- After moving the vocab topic: no dangling references in tone twins, tone practice, audio and review, and vocab progress with a saved `gs-numbers` entry does not break the vocab pages or stats (Task 2).
- A round never has duplicate questions, and a step with fewer than eight possible questions gives a shorter round, not an error (Task 3).
- A finished round records exactly once; leaving mid-round records answers but not a step score (Tasks 4, 6).
- Unknown step id shows a friendly page (Task 5).
- Old stored stats without the `numbers` key load cleanly (Task 4).

Ids are unique (`${entry.id}:${kind}`). The round is a seeded shuffle truncated to `size`; two different seeds give different orders for steps with enough questions.
- `gradeNumbers`: for `to-numeral` and `hear`, strip spaces, commas and a leading "₦", then "right" when equal to the single answer else "wrong"; for the others, `gradeTransform(input, q.answers, mode)`. Empty input is "wrong".
